The psychology

They feel before they think.

“Let’s get this policy signed.”
Guard upStall. Pushback. No.
“I’m excited to walk this journey with you! What are your thoughts on starting your savings program around $500 a month?”
Door openQuestions. Interest. Yes.

The feeling shows up first. The logic shows up second, and it defends the feeling.

The psychology

Why good words work.

01
Stories sell, facts tell
Policies, IUL, insurance, annuity sound like you’re selling them a product, and no one wants to be sold. Telling a 2 minute story of how your life has changed emotionally enrolls them in our crusade.
02
Make it about them, not you
Sell, close, recruit are things we do to them. Own, secure, get started are things they get.
03
Side by side, not below
Upline, downline, sideline, join, my team puts them at the bottom. Mentor, partner, our team, the team, friends, colleagues puts them next to us.

The closing question

Ask for the yes.

“Would you be open to…”
“…looking at different ways to optimize your current plan?”
“…a complimentary review of your current portfolio to make sure you reach your retirement goal while minimizing taxes?”
“…moving your 401k to a place where it’s completely protected from any market loss?”
“…starting your savings program on the 1st of the month, or would you rather wait until the 15th?”
